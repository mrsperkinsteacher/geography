Geography Sources

Reliable, free sources for current geography news, research and case studies - all clickable, no paywall. Use them when you need a current example for an assessment, a case study nobody else will use, or just to understand what is happening in the world through a geography lens. This site uses them to find stories for This Week in Geography.

How to find a good case study

Search the source's website for your topic - e.g. coral bleaching, food security Australia or South China Sea.

Look for articles from the last 2-3 years. The best case studies are specific - a named place, a real event, with data. Avoid articles that are just opinion with no evidence.
