The Three Films Unit · Lesson 2 of 8

Finish & Reflect

Students finish watching their film and complete their viewing notes. Early finishers begin thinking about their big question.

45 minutes Pairs ClickView required Year 9
What happens this lesson
1
Finish watching
Up to 35 min

Pick up where you left off. Keep adding to your viewing notes from last lesson as you go.

Finished early? (Drowning in Plastic pairs)
Move straight to Tasks 2 and 3 below while your classmates finish. You're ahead — use it.
2
Complete your viewing notes
10 min

Now that you've seen the whole film, add to or update what you wrote last lesson. Answer these two questions together with your partner:

What is the filmmaker's main argument?
Did the film change how you think about anything?
3
Your big question
5 min

Every good project starts with a question. Based on your film, write one question you genuinely want to find out more about. It could be about a place, a cause, a solution, or a decision someone made.

Examples: "Why is Australia one of the worst per-capita plastic users in the world?" or "What happened to the places in 2040 — did any of it actually work?"
