Records and fun facts - the high scores

8,849m

Tallest mountain

Mt Everest, Nepal/Tibet - measured to the summit above sea level.

10,984m

Deepest ocean point

Challenger Deep, Mariana Trench (2021 survey). Deeper than Everest is tall.

6,650km

Longest river

The Nile, Africa - though the Amazon gives it a close race.

30.37M km²

Largest continent

Africa. Bigger than the USA, China, India, Europe and Japan combined.

-89.2°C

Coldest temperature ever

Vostok Station, Antarctica, July 1983.

56.7°C

Hottest temperature ever

Furnace Creek, Death Valley, California, July 1913.

59,736km

Australia's coastline

Including its islands. The mainland alone is 35,877km (Geoscience Australia).

71%

Earth covered by ocean

And we've mapped only about 29% of the seafloor in detail (Seabed 2030, 2026). Most of it is still unknown.

195

Countries on Earth

193 UN member states plus Vatican City and Palestine.
