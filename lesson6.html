The Three Films Unit · Lesson 6 of 8

Plan Your Presentation

Before building, students plan every slide. A strong plan makes the build lesson fast and the presentation clear.

45 minutes Pairs MacBooks Year 9
Slide rules — read these first
1
What makes a good slide?
5 min
One idea per slide
Each slide has one clear point. If you are saying two things, make two slides.
No walls of text
Dot points only. Maximum 5 words per dot point. You say the rest out loud.
Use at least one visual
A map, a graph, a photo, or a data chart. Not clip art. Something real.
Every claim needs a source
If you say something is true, you need to show where you found it.
Your slide-by-slide plan
2
Plan every slide
30 min

Fill in the planner for each slide. Write what your key point is, what you will show visually, and what you will say. Use your notes from lessons 1–5.

Slide 1Title & Introduction
Your film title, your names, and one sentence about what your presentation is about.
Our title slide will say...
Slide 2The Problem
What environmental or social problem does your film reveal? Keep it specific.
Key point
Visual we will use
Slide 3Our SDG
Which SDG did you choose and why does it connect to your film? Include the SDG icon.
SDG and connection in one sentence
Slide 4Case Study
Your real-world case study. Named place, key facts, key statistic, source.
Place + key statistic
Visual (map or data chart)
Slide 5Australia's Role
What is Australia doing — and not doing — about this issue? Include Australia's SDG score.
What we found about Australia
Slide 6What Should Change
Your opinion, backed by your research. One specific thing that should change — and who should do it.
Our recommendation
3
Peer check
10 min

Swap your plan with another pair. Check each other's plans against these three questions:

1. Is every slide clear — can you tell what the key point is?
2. Is there at least one visual planned?
3. Is there a real case study with a specific place and statistic?

Give one piece of feedback before you swap back. Then adjust your plan if needed.
