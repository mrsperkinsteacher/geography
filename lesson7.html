The Three Films Unit · Lesson 7 of 8

Build Your Presentation

Students open PowerPoint and build from their plan. The plan is done — now it is about making it look good and say something real.

45 minutes Pairs MacBooks · PowerPoint Year 9
What happens this lesson
1
Slide design rules
5 min

Read these before you open PowerPoint.

Do
Use dot points of 5 words or fewer

Include at least one map, graph or real photo

Use a clean, readable font (not Comic Sans)

Put the source of any data or image in small text at the bottom of the slide

Leave white space — crowded slides are hard to read
Do not
Copy and paste paragraphs from websites

Use clip art or random internet images

Put everything you know on one slide

Use more than two font sizes

Forget to include your film title and SDG number
2
Build your slides
35 min

Open PowerPoint and work through your plan from lesson 6. Build each slide in order. Keep your plan open beside it.

Finding a map or visual
For maps: Google Maps, Google Earth, or NASA Earth Observatory. For data charts: Our World in Data has downloadable charts for almost every topic. Always note the source underneath.
  • Slide 1 — Title, film name, our names
  • Slide 2 — The problem, clearly described
  • Slide 3 — Our SDG with the SDG icon
  • Slide 4 — Case study with place, statistic, source
  • Slide 5 — Australia's role with SDG score
  • Slide 6 — What should change, and who should do it
  • At least one map, graph, or real photo somewhere in the presentation
  • Sources credited on the relevant slides
3
Finished early?
remaining time

Close your laptops and practise presenting to each other. One person presents while the other times. Each person should be able to explain their slides without reading from the screen. Aim for 3–4 minutes total.
