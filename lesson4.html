The Three Films Unit · Lesson 4 of 8

Find a Case Study

A guided research lesson. Students find one real-world case study that connects their film and SDG to a specific place.

45 minutes Pairs MacBooks Year 9
What happens this lesson
1
What makes a good case study?
5 min

A good case study has three things: a specific place, a real event or situation, and at least one piece of data or evidence. Vague is not useful.

Example — weak vs strong
Weak: "Plastic pollution is a problem in the ocean."

Strong: "Lord Howe Island, 600km east of NSW, has the highest recorded rate of plastic ingestion in seabirds globally — up to 90% of chicks on the island have plastic in their stomachs, according to a 2019 CSIRO study."

The strong version has a place, evidence, a source, and a statistic. That is what you are looking for.

2
Search for your case study
25 min

Use the Sources page to find a real case study that connects your film and your SDG to a specific place in the world. Use the section that matches your topic best.

Search tip: type your topic and a place name directly into the source website — not into Google. For example, on The Guardian's website search "coral bleaching Great Barrier Reef 2024" rather than searching Google.
