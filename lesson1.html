The Three Films Unit · Lesson 1 of 8

Watch & Notice

Choose your film, start watching, and record what you notice using the viewing guide below.

45 minutes Pairs ClickView required Year 9
What happens this lesson
1
Get set up
5 min

Open ClickView and find your film. One partner keeps this page open for the viewing notes below.

Write your names and your film choice at the top of the notes section.

Film 1
2040

G · 92 min · Australia, 2019

Open on ClickView ↗
Film 2
Before the Flood

PG · 96 min · Nat Geo, 2016

Search ClickView for the title.

Film 3
Drowning in Plastic

All ages · 60 min · BBC, 2018

Open on ClickView ↗
2
Watch — and take notes as you go
35 min

Watch your film. Use the four boxes below to record what you notice. You don't need to pause constantly — jot things down when something stands out.

There are no right answers here. The goal is to notice things, not to summarise everything.

The problem — what is it?
Where is it happening?
Who is affected?
Something that surprised me
3
Pause and discuss
5 min

With your partner, share one thing each that stood out so far. Then answer this together:

"What is the filmmaker trying to make us feel — and is it working?"

You don't need to write this down. Just talk about it.
