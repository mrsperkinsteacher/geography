The Three Films Unit · Lesson 3 of 8

Choose Your SDG

Students explore the UN Sustainable Development Goals, choose one that connects to their film, and explain the link.

45 minutes Pairs MacBooks Year 9
What happens this lesson
1
Explore the SDGs
10 min

Open the SDGs page. Click on at least five of the goal tiles and read what they are about. You are looking for one that connects to your film.

The suggested links below are a starting point — but you can choose any SDG you can justify.

2040
SDG 7 · 11 · 13 · 15
Clean energy, cities, climate, land
Before the Flood
SDG 7 · 13 · 15 · 17
Energy, climate, land, partnerships
Drowning in Plastic
SDG 6 · 12 · 14
Clean water, consumption, oceans
2
Read about your chosen SDG
10 min

Click through to the UN page for your chosen SDG. Read the goal description and at least three of the targets listed underneath it.

What are targets?
Each SDG has specific targets — the measurable steps countries have agreed to take to reach the goal by 2030. They tell you what success actually looks like.
Our chosen SDG
One target that matters to us
3
Make the connection
15 min

This is the most important part. Answer these three questions together. Write in full sentences — you will use these notes in your presentation later.

How does our film connect to this SDG?
What evidence from the film supports this?
Why does this SDG matter — who does it affect?
4
Commit
10 min

Share your SDG choice and connection with another pair. Explain it in two sentences. If you can't explain it clearly in two sentences, your connection might need to be tighter.

Our commitment
We are connecting our film to SDG ___

Lock this in. You will build your whole presentation around this choice.
