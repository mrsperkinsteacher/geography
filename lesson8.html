The Three Films Unit · Lesson 8 of 8

Share & Reflect

Pairs present to a small group, give and receive feedback, and write a personal reflection. The unit ends with a question, not an answer.

45 minutes Small groups of 3–4 pairs Year 9
What happens this lesson
1
Presentations in small groups
25 min

Each pair presents to your group. Aim for 3–4 minutes. You do not need to read from your slides — talk through them in your own words.

While you are listening to another group present, fill in the feedback card for them.

Feedback for: ________________________
One thing I found genuinely interesting
One question I still have after their presentation
Feedback for: ________________________
One thing I found genuinely interesting
One question I still have after their presentation
Feedback for: ________________________
One thing I found genuinely interesting
One question I still have after their presentation
2
Individual reflection
10 min

Do this alone. Answer honestly — there are no wrong answers and nobody else needs to read this unless you want them to.

What is one thing from your film or research that actually changed how you think?
Is there anything you will actually do differently because of this unit?
What question do you still have that this unit did not answer?
That's the unit.
You watched a film about something real. You found evidence. You made a connection to a global goal. You researched what Australia is actually doing. You built something and shared it. The question you wrote above — that one is yours to keep.
