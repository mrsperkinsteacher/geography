The Three Films Unit · Lesson 5 of 8

What About Australia?

Students research Australia's role in their issue — what we are doing, what we are not doing, and what we could do better.

45 minutes Pairs MacBooks Year 9
Teacher notes
This lesson asks students to bring the global back to the local. The question — what is Australia actually doing about this? — often surprises students. Australia has a complicated relationship with most SDG issues: a high per-capita carbon emitter, one of the world's biggest plastic producers, but also with real examples of action. Push students to find specific evidence rather than general statements. The SDG Index Dashboard at dashboards.sdgindex.org is excellent for this — students can look up Australia's actual score on their chosen SDG and compare it with other countries.
